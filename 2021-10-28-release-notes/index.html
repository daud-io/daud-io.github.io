2021.10.28 update

  • October 28, 2021October 28, 2021
  • Most of the input code has been rewritten. Mouse and keyboard should be more responsive and accurate.
  • Touchscreen support has a major new update. Tap any point on the screen to shoot at that point, then resume flying as before.
  • Minor physics changes, restoring friction on the balls and preserving angular rotation of abandoned ships.
  • Performance, faster FPS, less memory, less CPU
  • Settings: Bandwidth setting was not working previously, was always “high”. Lower settings work again.
  • Settings: New settings “Mouse Pointerlock” – this may help with clicking out of the window, on other tabs/windows, accidentally clicking buttons on the side of your mouse. Your mouse cursor will switch to a red targeting reticle in-game.
